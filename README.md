# Lisbon Spatial Dynamics

A longitudinal spatial study of how Lisbon's neighbourhoods have changed over time through housing markets, population, mobility, tourism, and urban infrastructure.

## Research objective

The project studies spatial and temporal changes across Lisbon, with a particular focus on how neighbourhood-level housing dynamics relate to broader changes in urban activity and structure.

The intended analysis combines reproducible public datasets at the finest consistent spatial resolution available, prioritising official sources and transparent statistical methods.

## Planned dimensions

- housing prices and transaction activity;
- population and demographic change;
- active and public mobility;
- tourism and local accommodation;
- urban infrastructure and accessibility;
- neighbourhood-level spatial structure.

## Status

Early research and data-source design.
