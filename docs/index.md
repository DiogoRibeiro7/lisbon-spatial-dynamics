## Project scope

The project is designed as a longitudinal spatial study of Lisbon. Its central unit of analysis will be chosen at the finest geographic level that can be supported consistently across datasets and years.

The first research phase focuses on building a reliable spatial panel rather than fitting a model prematurely. Housing acts as the anchor outcome, while mobility, demographic, tourism, and infrastructure variables provide context for interpreting change.

## Research principles

The project follows four principles:

- **comparability before granularity** — a slightly coarser geography is preferable to inconsistent boundaries;
- **official sources first** — public administrative and statistical sources are preferred when suitable;
- **provenance is part of the data** — every derived dataset should retain source, period, geographic unit, and transformation metadata;
- **description before causality** — spatial association and temporal co-movement are not treated as causal effects without an appropriate design.

## Current status

The repository is in its foundation phase. The immediate work is to define the data catalogue, spatial units, ingestion contracts, and reproducible validation pipeline before substantive analysis begins.
